Simple Process

How to Apply

Four easy steps to enroll your child at The Meridian School Tordher.

1. Contact Us

Call us at 0316 6388855 or visit our campus on Main Road Tordher to schedule a visit.

2. Fill Application

Complete the admission form at the school office or submit an online inquiry below.

3. Submit Documents

Provide the required documents — birth certificate, photos, and previous school record (if any).

4. Confirmation

Receive admission confirmation and welcome your child to The Meridian family!

What to Bring

Birth Certificate

Original NADRA Form-B / Birth Certificate of the child, with a photocopy.

Photographs

4 recent passport-sized photographs of the child.

Parent CNIC

Photocopy of father's / guardian's CNIC (both sides).

Previous Record

School Leaving Certificate or last report card (for Class 1 and above).
