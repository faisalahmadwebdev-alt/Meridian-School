Welcome to Meridian

A Foundation for Lifelong Learning

The Meridian School Tordher is an active private educational institution officially registered under the Private Schools Regulatory Authority (PSRA) of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Swabi District.

Our early-childhood and primary branch provides specialized foundational learning — from Playgroup through Class 5 — focusing on cognitive development, spatial awareness, and interactive, hands-on learning experiences.

Our Programs

Classes We Offer

From your child's first classroom experience to a strong primary foundation — we've got every step covered.

Playgroup

A warm, playful introduction to school life with sensory activities, colors, rhymes, and social interaction.

Age: 2.5 – 3.5 years

Nursery

Building on curiosity — early literacy, numeracy, and creative expression through structured play.

Age: 3.5 – 4.5 years

Kindergarten (KG)

Preparing confident learners for formal schooling with phonics, basic math, and social skills.

Age: 4.5 – 5.5 years

Primary (Class 1–5)

English, Urdu, Mathematics, General Science, Islamiyat, and Social Studies — building strong academic foundations.

Class 1 to Class 5
