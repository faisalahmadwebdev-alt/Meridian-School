Programs

Our Academic Programs

Each stage of learning is designed to match your child's developmental needs.

Playgroup

Introduction to school routines through play, colors, shapes, rhymes, and sensory exploration. Focus on socialization and motor skills.

Age: 2.5 – 3.5 years

Nursery

Early literacy and numeracy through engaging, hands-on activities. Phonics, counting, storytelling, and creative art.

Age: 3.5 – 4.5 years

Kindergarten

Preparing confident learners for Class 1 with phonics mastery, basic math, reading, and structured classroom habits.

Age: 4.5 – 5.5 years

Primary (Class 1–5)

Full academic curriculum including English, Urdu, Mathematics, General Science, Islamiyat, and Social Studies.

Class 1 to Class 5
Subjects

Curriculum Overview

Class Core Subjects Focus Areas
Playgroup Rhymes, Colors, Shapes, Play Motor skills, socialization, sensory learning
Nursery English, Urdu, Numbers, Art Phonics, counting, storytelling, creativity
Kindergarten English, Urdu, Math, Islamiyat Reading, writing, basic arithmetic, discipline
Class 1–2 English, Urdu, Math, Science, Islamiyat Reading comprehension, grammar, problem-solving
Class 3–5 English, Urdu, Math, Science, Islamiyat, Social Studies Advanced concepts, exam preparation, critical thinking
Teaching Approach

How We Teach at Meridian

Activity-Based

Lessons delivered through play, projects, and interactive activities for deeper understanding.

Cognitive Development

Special focus on reasoning, problem-solving, and early mathematical concepts.

Multi-Sensory

Engaging sight, sound, touch, and movement to reinforce learning in every child.

Small Groups

Focused attention ensuring no child is left behind and every voice is heard.
